# Your path (cut from 30 Days of Python)

Official course: [Asabeneh / 30 Days of Python](https://github.com/Asabeneh/30-Days-Of-Python).

The course itself says this can take longer than 30 days. We follow that.
Pace: 5–8 hours a week. One topic per session. Do not skip ahead.

Day 1 in the original course tours every data type in one sitting.
We do not. Each type gets the session already assigned to it below.

## How a session ends

1. You explain the concept in your own words.
2. You commit: `session-XX: <what you learned>`
3. We name the next topic. We do not start it.
